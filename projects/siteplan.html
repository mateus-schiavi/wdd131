Website Planning Document

Capoeira – The Afro-Brazilian Martial Art

Site Name

Capoeira Roots

This name was chosen because it represents the historical and cultural roots of capoeira, highlighting its Afro-Brazilian origin and traditions.

Optional domain: capoeira-roots.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of this site is to introduce visitors to capoeira, explaining its history, cultural significance, and physical and mental health benefits. The site will also encourage users to engage with capoeira through interactive content and a sign-up form.

Scenarios

  • What is capoeira and where did it originate?
  • What physical and mental benefits can I gain from practicing capoeira?

Color Schema

  • Dark Green (#1B5E20) – headings, navigation, accents
  • Warm Yellow (#FBC02D) – highlights, buttons, accents
  • Light Gray (#F5F5F5) – page background

Typography

  • Poppins – Headings
  • Roboto – Body text
